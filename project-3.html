Project Overview

As a Reactor Design and Undergrad Lab Assistant at the Iowa State Bioeconomic Institute (2022-2025), I designed hydrotreater systems for advanced fuel processing applications. I was responsible for the maintenance, troubleshooting, and testing of pyrolysis reactors used in bioeconomic research. I developed comprehensive operating procedures to address critical safety concerns associated with hydrogen-based reactions.

I reviewed fuel processing techniques to identify optimization opportunities and documented experimental results with meticulous accuracy to support data analysis and scientific reporting. This work contributed to ongoing bioeconomic research and process optimization for sustainable fuel production.
